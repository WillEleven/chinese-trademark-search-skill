# California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Notice / 加州消费者隐私法通知

**Effective Date:** 2026-03-28
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-28

---

## English

This notice is provided to California residents pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (collectively, "CCPA"), Cal. Civ. Code sections 1798.100 et seq.

### We Do Not Sell Personal Information

**The platform does not sell, and has not sold in the preceding 12 months, any personal information of its users.** The platform does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising purposes.

### Categories of Personal Information Collected

| Category (per CCPA section 1798.140) | Examples | Collected |
|---|---|---|
| Identifiers | User ID, email address, account name, IP address | Yes |
| Commercial information | Points purchase history, transaction records | Yes |
| Internet or electronic network activity | Query history, search terms, pages accessed, API usage logs | Yes |
| Professional or employment-related information | Organization name, business role (if provided) | Yes |
| Geolocation data | Approximate location derived from IP address | Yes |
| Sensitive personal information | N/A | No |

### Business Purpose for Collection

Personal information is collected and used for the following business purposes:

1. **Providing the service** - Processing trademark search queries, delivering results, managing user accounts
2. **Billing and account management** - Points balance tracking, transaction processing
3. **Security and fraud prevention** - Detecting unauthorized access, preventing abuse
4. **Compliance** - Sanctions screening, audit trail maintenance, responding to legal obligations
5. **Service improvement** - Analyzing usage patterns to improve search quality and platform reliability (aggregated and anonymized)

### Your Rights Under the CCPA

As a California resident, you have the following rights:

| Right | Description |
|---|---|
| **Right to Know** | You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you, the sources of that information, the business purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties with whom it is shared. |
| **Right to Delete** | You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., legal compliance, completing transactions). |
| **Right to Opt-Out of Sale** | As we do not sell personal information, this right is not applicable. However, you may still submit a request and we will confirm our non-sale status. |
| **Right to Correct** | You may request correction of inaccurate personal information. |
| **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive PI** | We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CCPA. |

### Non-Discrimination

The platform will **not** discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights. You will not receive:
- A different level or quality of service
- Different pricing or rates
- A suggestion that you will receive a different level of service or pricing

### How to Submit a Request

To exercise any of your CCPA rights, contact:

**Email:** privacy@zqaiip.com

**Subject line:** "CCPA Request - [Right to Know / Right to Delete / Other]"

Please include:
- Your full name and email address associated with your account
- The specific right you wish to exercise
- Sufficient information for us to verify your identity

We will acknowledge your request within 10 business days and provide a substantive response within 45 calendar days. If additional time is needed, we will notify you of the extension (up to an additional 45 days).

### Authorized Agents

You may designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. The authorized agent must provide written authorization signed by you, and we may require identity verification from both you and the agent.

### Data Retention

Query logs and associated personal information are retained for 12 months. See [API_TERMS_OF_USE.md](API_TERMS_OF_USE.md) for detailed retention schedules.

## 中文

本通知根据 2018 年《加州消费者隐私法》（经 2020 年《加州隐私权法》修订，统称"CCPA"）向加州居民提供，见《加州民法典》第 1798.100 条及以下各条。

### 我们不出售个人信息

**本平台不出售、且在过去 12 个月内未出售过任何用户的个人信息。** 本平台不会为跨情境行为广告目的共享个人信息。

### 收集的个人信息类别

| 类别（依据 CCPA 第 1798.140 条） | 示例 | 是否收集 |
|---|---|---|
| 标识符 | 用户 ID、电子邮箱、账户名、IP 地址 | 是 |
| 商业信息 | 点数购买记录、交易记录 | 是 |
| 互联网或电子网络活动信息 | 查询历史、检索词、访问的页面、API 使用日志 | 是 |
| 职业或就业相关信息 | 组织名称、业务角色（如提供） | 是 |
| 地理位置数据 | 基于 IP 地址的大致位置 | 是 |
| 敏感个人信息 | 不适用 | 否 |

### 收集的商业目的

个人信息的收集和使用基于以下商业目的：

1. **提供服务** - 处理商标查询请求、交付结果、管理用户账户
2. **计费和账户管理** - 点数余额跟踪、交易处理
3. **安全和反欺诈** - 检测未授权访问、防止滥用
4. **合规** - 制裁筛查、审计追踪维护、履行法律义务
5. **服务改进** - 分析使用模式以提升查询质量和平台可靠性（聚合和匿名化）

### 您在 CCPA 下的权利

作为加州居民，您享有以下权利：

| 权利 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| **知情权** | 您可以请求披露收集的个人信息类别和具体内容、信息来源、收集的商业目的，以及与之共享的第三方类别。 |
| **删除权** | 您可以请求删除您的个人信息，但须受某些例外情况限制（如法律合规、完成交易）。 |
| **拒绝出售权** | 由于我们不出售个人信息，此权利不适用。但您仍可提交请求，我们将确认我们的非出售状态。 |
| **更正权** | 您可以请求更正不准确的个人信息。 |
| **限制敏感个人信息使用权** | 我们不收集 CCPA 定义的敏感个人信息。 |

### 非歧视

本平台**不会**因您行使 CCPA 权利而对您进行歧视。您不会因此受到：
- 不同水平或质量的服务
- 不同的定价或费率
- 暗示您将接受不同水平的服务或定价

### 如何提交请求

如需行使您的 CCPA 权利，请联系：

**电子邮件：** privacy@zqaiip.com

**邮件主题：** "CCPA Request - [知情权 / 删除权 / 其他]"

请包含以下信息：
- 与您账户关联的全名和电子邮箱
- 您希望行使的具体权利
- 足以验证您身份的信息

我们将在 10 个工作日内确认收到您的请求，并在 45 个日历日内提供实质性回复。如需额外时间，我们将通知您延期情况（最多再延长 45 天）。

### 授权代理人

您可以指定授权代理人代表您提交请求。授权代理人必须提供您签署的书面授权，我们可能需要同时验证您和代理人的身份。

### 数据保留

查询日志及相关个人信息保留 12 个月。详细保留计划请参阅 [API_TERMS_OF_USE.md](API_TERMS_OF_USE.md)。
