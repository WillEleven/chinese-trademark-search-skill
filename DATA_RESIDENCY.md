## English

### Overview

The platform currently operates a **single-region deployment** in mainland China, with the unified entry point `tm.zhengquai.com`. An overseas region is planned but not yet available.

### Current Region: China Mainland

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Entry Point** | `tm.zhengquai.com` |
| **Data Storage Location** | Mainland China |
| **Applicable Data Protection Law** | Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (PIPL) |
| **Intended Users** | All users (single-region deployment) |
| **Regulatory Authority** | Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) |

Data stored in the China mainland region is subject to PIPL and related regulations, including the Data Security Law (DSL) and the Cybersecurity Law (CSL). Cross-border data transfer from this region is subject to regulatory approval mechanisms as required by Chinese law.

### Planned Region: Overseas

An overseas region with independent data storage is planned. Until it is available, all users — including international users — are served by the mainland China deployment described above. This document will be updated when the overseas region launches.

### Notice for EU Users

Because the platform currently operates only the mainland China region, personal data of EU users is stored in mainland China and is subject to PIPL. Hong Kong or other overseas nodes are not yet available. EU users should review [GDPR_COMPLIANCE.md](GDPR_COMPLIANCE.md) for details on cross-border transfer safeguards before using the service.

### Right to Erasure

Users may request erasure of their personal data in accordance with applicable law. To submit an erasure request, contact:

**lengqifeng11@gmail.com**

Please include your user ID and registered email in your request. Erasure requests will be processed within 30 days, subject to any legal retention obligations.

## 中文

### 概述

本平台当前为**单区域部署**（中国大陆），统一入口为 `tm.zhengquai.com`。海外节点在规划中，尚未上线。

### 当前区域：中国大陆

| 属性 | 详情 |
|---|---|
| **入口** | `tm.zhengquai.com` |
| **数据存储位置** | 中国大陆 |
| **适用数据保护法律** | 《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》（PIPL） |
| **目标用户** | 全部用户（单区域部署） |
| **监管机构** | 国家互联网信息办公室（CAC） |

中国大陆区域存储的数据受 PIPL 及相关法规约束，包括《数据安全法》（DSL）和《网络安全法》（CSL）。从该区域进行跨境数据传输须遵守中国法律规定的监管审批机制。

### 规划中区域：海外节点

具备独立数据存储的海外节点在规划中。在其上线之前，包括国际用户在内的所有用户均由上述中国大陆部署提供服务。海外节点上线后本文档将同步更新。

### 对欧盟用户的说明

由于平台当前仅运营中国大陆区域，欧盟用户的个人数据存储在中国大陆并受 PIPL 约束，暂无香港或其他海外节点可选。欧盟用户在使用本服务前，请参阅 [GDPR_COMPLIANCE.md](GDPR_COMPLIANCE.md) 了解跨境传输保障措施详情。
